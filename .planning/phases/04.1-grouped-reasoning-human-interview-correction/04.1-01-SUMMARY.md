## Decisions Made

- Used a compatibility coercion layer instead of keeping root-level `top_3` in the strict schema, so existing internal fallback paths still normalize while the LLM-facing contract stays grouped and explicit.
- Kept the shared `apply_final_meal_resolution()` transaction authoritative and inserted a grouped adapter ahead of it, which preserves FoodVisual learning and correction invalidation behavior without redesigning the writer.
- Skipped raw-segment completion rows in polling whenever authoritative `meal_entries` exist, so grouped writes and user-visible summaries stay aligned.
