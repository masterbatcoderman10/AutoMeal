| post-interview grounding worker -> authoritative final write | External-provider/tool failures cross into the save path and must not strand or corrupt confirmed meal state. |

| T-04.3-06-02 | Repudiation | `app/services/interview_service.py` | mitigate | Persist correction evidence, ordered action progress, and final degraded/hold grounding state in structured interview payloads so REASON-04 shows exactly why the meal advanced or held. |
