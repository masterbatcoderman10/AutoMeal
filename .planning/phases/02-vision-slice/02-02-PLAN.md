<threat_model>
| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Raw Gemini-style box coordinates are stored without normalization | HIGH | Convert `0..1000` provider coordinates to `[0,1]` floats before validation or persistence |
| Crop files are written outside the uploads volume or with inconsistent paths | MEDIUM | Centralize crop saves in `image_service.py` and hard-assert `/data/uploads/crops/{segment_id}.jpg` |
| Labeling is attempted on unvalidated regions | MEDIUM | Only create label prompts after segment validation and crop creation succeed |
| Result message exposes debugging detail or counts every tiny item | LOW | Format one plain sentence from accepted grouped labels only |
</threat_model>
