## Latest Human Finding

Status: fail, improved but not acceptable.

- Reasoning incorrectly grouped pita bread with curry. Food groups must be isolated foods/components, not compound plate pairings, unless the foods are physically integrated.
- Bread identity needs sharper reasoning because chapatti/chapati, parota/paratha, khubz, pita, naan, and roti can change nutrition materially.
- Vector candidates appeared despite the operator expecting an empty/no-match state. When no vector match has happened, the prompt context must explicitly say no usable vector candidates are available and must not include fake placeholder candidates.
