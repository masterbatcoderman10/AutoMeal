## Shared Patterns

- State durability and replay are centralized in `interview_service.persist_interview_step()` (`735-777`) and reused by both callback and text handlers.
- Deterministic ordering is controlled by `question_order` + `remaining_required_question_ids` (`2104-2143`) and updated atomically in `_apply_clarification_answer` (`2277-2310`).
- Callback token generation/lookup uses `callback_token` + `resolve_callback_token` from `bot/callback_data.py` and is already contract-tested in both bot handlers and tests.
- Source-origin answers are normalized at parse-time (`interview_service._parse_clarification_text`) and preserved through `confirmation_items` assembly (`2171-2200`, `2430-2443`).
